# Tomates à la Provençale

- **Source:** French Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** accompagnements
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 ripe garden tomatoes, halved
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons breadcrumbs
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Season tomato halves, cut side up, with salt and pepper.
2. Mix breadcrumbs, garlic, parsley, and 1 tablespoon of the olive oil into a light topping; press onto the cut side of
   each tomato half.
3. Heat remaining olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat.
4. Place tomatoes cut-side up in the skillet and cook 8-10 minutes until softened and the topping is golden (finish
   under a broiler for a couple minutes if you want more color).
5. Serve warm.

## Notes

Built specifically to use up a garden tomato glut. Very simple — the tomato itself stays the star, with just enough
garlic and breadcrumb to add texture.
