# Riz Simple (Simple Rice)

- **Source:** French Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** accompagnements
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water or broth
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Rinse rice until water runs mostly clear.
2. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat, add rice, and stir 1 minute to lightly toast.
3. Add water/broth and a pinch of salt, bring to a boil, then cover and reduce to low heat.
4. Simmer 15-18 minutes until liquid is absorbed, then rest covered 5 minutes off heat before fluffing.

## Notes

The standard rice side for escalope-de-poulet-panee.md or any simple main — plain on purpose.
