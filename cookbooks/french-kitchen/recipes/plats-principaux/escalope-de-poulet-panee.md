# Escalope de Poulet Panée (Breaded Chicken Cutlet)

- **Source:** French Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** plats principaux
- **Servings:** 2
- **Prep/cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 3-4 tablespoons olive oil, for frying
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Instructions

1. Season chicken slices with salt and pepper.
2. Dredge each piece in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, coating fully at each stage.
3. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat.
4. Fry cutlets 3-4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
5. Serve with a squeeze of lemon.
