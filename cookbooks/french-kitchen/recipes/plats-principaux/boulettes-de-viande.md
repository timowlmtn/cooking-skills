# Boulettes de Viande (French-Style Meatballs)

- **Source:** French Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** plats principaux
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (or beef/pork mix)
- 1 small shallot, finely minced
- 1/3 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Combine ground beef, shallot, breadcrumbs, egg, mustard, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix just until combined.
2. Roll into meatballs (about 1.5 inches).
3. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Sear meatballs, turning occasionally, until browned all over
   and cooked through, about 10-12 minutes.
4. Serve with pan juices spooned over — no additional sauce needed.

## Notes

Simple, herb-forward meatballs with no heavy sauce, keeping the meat the focus. Good alongside tomates-provencale.md.
