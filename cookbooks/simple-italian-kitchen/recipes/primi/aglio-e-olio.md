# Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** primi
- **Servings:** 2
- **Prep/cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- Salt, to taste
- Grated Parmesan, optional

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook spaghetti until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before
   draining.
2. While pasta cooks, warm olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking
   gently until garlic is golden — do not let it brown or burn.
3. Add drained pasta to the pan along with a splash of reserved pasta water. Toss to coat, adding more pasta water as
   needed for a light sauce.
4. Stir in parsley and season with salt.
5. Serve immediately, with Parmesan if desired.

## Notes

Garlic burns fast — pull the pan off heat a moment early if it's coloring quickly.
