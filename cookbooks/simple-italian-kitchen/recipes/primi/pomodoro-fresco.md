# Pasta al Pomodoro Fresco

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** primi
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 400g pasta (penne or spaghetti)
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
- A few fresh basil leaves, torn
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Grated Parmesan, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook 1 minute more, until fragrant.
3. Pour in crushed tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Meanwhile, cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente; drain.
5. Toss pasta with the sauce, add torn basil, and adjust seasoning.
6. Serve topped with grated Parmesan.

## Notes

A pinch of sugar can balance overly acidic canned tomatoes.
