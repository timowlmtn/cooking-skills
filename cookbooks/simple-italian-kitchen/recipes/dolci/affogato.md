# Affogato

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** dolci
- **Servings:** 1
- **Prep/cook time:** 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 scoop vanilla gelato or ice cream
- 1 shot (about 30ml) hot espresso
- Optional: a few coffee beans or a biscotti, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Place the scoop of gelato in a small glass or cup.
2. Brew the espresso hot, immediately pour it over the gelato.
3. Serve right away, with a biscotti on the side if desired.

## Notes

Simplest dessert in the archive — good for finishing off a bigger meal without extra effort.
