# Bruschetta al Pomodoro

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** antipasti
- **Servings:** 4 as a starter
- **Prep/cook time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
- 2 cloves garlic (1 minced, 1 whole)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
- 1 tablespoon fresh basil, chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 small baguette or Italian loaf, sliced

## Instructions

1. Toast or grill the bread slices until golden and crisp.
2. While still warm, rub one side of each slice with the whole garlic clove.
3. In a bowl, combine diced tomatoes, minced garlic, olive oil, basil, salt, and pepper.
4. Spoon the tomato mixture onto the toasted bread just before serving.
5. Drizzle with a little extra olive oil.

## Notes

Salt the tomatoes and let them sit 5 minutes first if they're watery — drain off excess liquid so the bread doesn't get
soggy.
