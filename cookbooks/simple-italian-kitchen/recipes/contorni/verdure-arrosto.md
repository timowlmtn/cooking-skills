# Verdure Arrosto (Roasted Vegetables)

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** contorni
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 small eggplant, cubed
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Toss all vegetables with olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper on a baking sheet.
3. Spread in a single layer and roast 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and lightly charred.
4. Serve warm as a side.

## Notes

Good alongside pollo al limone (secondi/pollo-al-limone.md) or on its own with crusty bread.
