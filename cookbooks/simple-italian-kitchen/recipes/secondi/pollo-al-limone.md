# Pollo al Limone

- **Source:** Simple Italian Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** secondi
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or thighs)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup flour, for dredging
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons butter
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

## Instructions

1. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then dredge lightly in flour, shaking off excess.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook chicken until golden and cooked through, about 4-5
   minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
3. Lower heat to medium, add butter, lemon juice, and chicken broth to the pan, scraping up any browned bits.
4. Simmer 3-4 minutes until slightly reduced.
5. Return chicken to the pan, spoon sauce over top, and warm through.
6. Sprinkle with parsley before serving.

## Notes

Pairs well with the roasted vegetables (contorni/verdure-arrosto.md) or a simple side of rice.
