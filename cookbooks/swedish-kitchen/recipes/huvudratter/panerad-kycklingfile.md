# Panerad Kycklingfilé (Breaded Fried Chicken Breast)

- **Source:** Swedish Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** huvudrätter
- **Servings:** 2
- **Prep/cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 3-4 tablespoons olive oil, for frying
- Lemon wedges, for serving

## Instructions

1. Season chicken slices with salt and pepper.
2. Set up a dredging line: flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs. Coat each piece fully at each stage.
3. Heat olive oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat until shimmering.
4. Fry cutlets 3-4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
5. Rest briefly on a rack or paper towel, then serve with a squeeze of lemon.

## Notes

This is Tim's go-to quick dish — breaded, fried chicken breast in olive oil. Serve with plain rice on the side, same as
usual.
