# Pressgurka (Quick-Pickled Cucumber Salad)

- **Source:** Swedish Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** tillbehör
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 10 minutes plus 15 minutes resting

## Ingredients

- 1 English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons white vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 3 tablespoons water
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh dill or parsley, chopped, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Whisk vinegar, sugar, water, and a pinch of salt until sugar dissolves.
2. Pour over cucumber slices and toss to coat.
3. Let sit at least 15 minutes at room temperature or in the fridge.
4. Garnish with dill or parsley before serving.

## Notes

A light, fresh side that cuts through richer mains like kottbullar.md. Very simple — no cooking required.
