# Dillpotatis (Dill Potatoes)

- **Source:** Swedish Kitchen (original), page N/A
- **Season/category:** tillbehör
- **Servings:** 4
- **Prep/cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs small new potatoes, halved if large
- Salt, for boiling water
- 2 tablespoons butter or olive oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill, chopped

## Instructions

1. Boil potatoes in generously salted water until fork-tender, about 12-15 minutes.
2. Drain well.
3. Toss with butter (or olive oil) and fresh dill while still hot.
4. Season with additional salt to taste and serve immediately.
