## Notes

Not a transcription of an existing published cookbook — an original starter set of simple Swedish home-cooking dishes,
built around how Tim actually cooks: chicken breast, olive oil, cast-iron skillet, and simple flavors where the protein
is the star rather than a heavy sauce. Traditional versions of these dishes often lean on butter and cream gravies; this
set keeps things lighter and simpler to match that preference. Edit, replace, or expand freely, including swapping in
recipes from an actual Swedish cookbook later if one is picked up.
