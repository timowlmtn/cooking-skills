# Cooking Preferences

Last updated: 2026-08-17

A standing profile of how Tim actually cooks, so the `meal-plan` skill (and anyone browsing `cookbooks/`) doesn't have to re-ask every time.

## Go-to staples

- **Protein:** chicken breast, most often
- **Fat:** olive oil
- **Method:** cast-iron skillet
- **Favorite quick dish:** breaded, fried chicken breast slices in olive oil, with rice on the side
- **Also loves:** meatballs, and the occasional pizza with store-bought crust

## Flavor philosophy

Prefers simple flavors where the food itself is the star, not the sauce. Avoids complex, heavily-sauced dishes as a rule.

**Exception:** Italian food — comfortable with store-bought Italian sauce or canned fire-roasted tomatoes as a base.

## Current garden situation

Abundant fresh tomatoes as of 2026-08-17 — see `pantry.md` seasonal note. Favor tomato-forward recipes while this lasts.

## How to use this

When running the `meal-plan` skill, weight recipe choices toward these staples/method and toward simpler preparations first, reserving heavier sauces for Italian dishes. Update this file whenever preferences change.
