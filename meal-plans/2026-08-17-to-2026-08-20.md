## Quick cook (phone-ready)

### Monday, 2026-08-17 — Escalope de Poulet Panée + Riz Simple

- 2 chicken breasts, sliced into cutlets
- Salt, pepper
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1 egg, beaten
- 3/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 3-4 tbsp olive oil
- Lemon wedges
- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water or broth
- 1 tbsp olive oil (for rice)

1. Season chicken; dredge flour → egg → breadcrumbs.
2. Heat oil in cast iron, fry cutlets 3-4 min/side until golden.
3. Rest briefly, squeeze lemon over, serve.
4. Rice: toast rice in oil 1 min, add water + pinch salt, boil, cover, simmer 15-18 min, rest 5 min, fluff.

### Tuesday, 2026-08-18 — Pollo al Limone + Verdure Arrosto

- 4 chicken breasts (or thighs)
- Salt, pepper, 1/4 cup flour
- 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp butter
- Juice of 2 lemons, 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 1 zucchini, 1 red bell pepper, 1 eggplant, 1 red onion
- 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp oregano, salt, pepper

1. Oven to 425°F. Toss vegetables with oil, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper; roast 25-30 min, turning halfway.
2. Season chicken, dredge in flour.
3. Sear in olive oil 4-5 min/side, remove.
4. Add butter, lemon juice, broth to pan; simmer 3-4 min, scraping bits.
5. Return chicken, spoon sauce over, sprinkle parsley.

### Wednesday, 2026-08-19 — Boulettes de Viande + Tomates à la Provençale

- 1 lb ground beef, 1 shallot (minced), 1/3 cup breadcrumbs, 1 egg, 1 tbsp Dijon, 1 tbsp parsley, salt, pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil (for frying)
- 4 garden tomatoes, halved
- 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 cloves garlic, 2 tbsp breadcrumbs, 1 tbsp parsley, salt, pepper

1. Mix beef, shallot, breadcrumbs, egg, Dijon, parsley, salt, pepper. Roll into meatballs.
2. Sear in cast iron with olive oil, turning, ~10-12 min until cooked through.
3. Season tomato halves; press on garlic/breadcrumb/parsley topping.
4. Pan-cook tomatoes cut-side up in olive oil, 8-10 min until soft and golden.

### Thursday, 2026-08-20 — Bruschetta al Pomodoro + Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

- 4 tomatoes, diced, 2 garlic cloves (1 minced, 1 whole), 2 tbsp olive oil + extra, 1 tbsp basil, salt, pepper, baguette sliced
- 200g spaghetti, 4 garlic cloves (sliced), 1/3 cup olive oil, 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, 2 tbsp parsley, salt, Parmesan (optional)

1. Toast bread; rub warm slices with whole garlic clove.
2. Mix diced tomato, minced garlic, oil, basil, salt, pepper. Spoon onto bread, drizzle oil.
3. Boil spaghetti; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, drain.
4. Warm oil with sliced garlic and chili flakes over medium-low until golden — don't burn.
5. Toss pasta in with a splash of pasta water; stir in parsley, season, serve.
